I’m a Research Engineer in Robotics at the [Autonomous and Kooperative Systems Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/akslab/home), University of Tartu, Estonia, advised by [Prof. Arun Kumar Singh](https://tuit.ut.ee/en/content/arun-kumar-singh). My work focuses on robot learning, motion planning under uncertainty, risk-aware decision making, and reinforcement learning for reliable real-world autonomy.

Previously, I was a Project Associate and Visiting Student at [Moonlab, IISER Bhopal](https://moonlab.iiserb.ac.in/), advised by [Prof. Sujit P. B.](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qqwyAwoAAAAJ&hl=en). There, I worked on localization-constrained planning and learning-based swarm fragmentation.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Production Engineering from [Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI)](https://vjti.ac.in/), Mumbai, in May 2025.
