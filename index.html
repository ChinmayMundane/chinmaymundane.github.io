Chinmay Mundane

I’m a Research Engineer in Robotics at the Autonomous and Kooperative Systems Lab, University of Tartu, Estonia, advised by Prof. Arun Kumar Singh. My work focuses on robot learning, motion planning under uncertainty, risk-aware decision making, and reinforcement learning for reliable real-world autonomy.

Previously, I was a Project Associate and Visiting Student at Moonlab, IISER Bhopal, advised by Prof. Sujit P. B.. There, I worked on localization-constrained planning and learning-based swarm fragmentation.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Production Engineering from Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI), Mumbai, in May 2025.
